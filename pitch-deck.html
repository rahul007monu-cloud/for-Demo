But today's parents choose schools on Google and WhatsApp. Newer schools look more modern online, even without your experience. This plan closes that gap.

Common Challenges Today

💰 Late Fee Payments

Calling parents every month, manual registers, and no clear view of pending dues.

📞 Lost Admission Enquiries

Phone enquiries are noted in a diary and often never followed up.

📋 Heavy Office Workload

Receipts, TCs, certificates and report cards are all prepared by hand.

📢 Reaching Parents

Holidays, PTMs and homework go through diaries and scattered WhatsApp groups.

Our Core Solution

1. Fee Management

Online UPI payments, automatic WhatsApp reminders, instant receipts and a pending-dues dashboard.

2. Admission System

Online form on the website. Every enquiry gets an instant WhatsApp reply and automatic follow-ups.

3. Attendance Alerts

Teachers mark attendance on mobile. Parents are alerted immediately if their child is absent.

4. Results & Report Cards

Enter marks once and RBSE-format PDF report cards are generated automatically.

5. New Website + Google

Modern 3D website, AI chat assistant, and a strong Google profile so parents find you on "school near me".

6. Certificates

TC, bonafide and character certificates in one click.
