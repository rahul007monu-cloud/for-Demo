About JVM

A School Built on Values, Driven by Excellence

Jagriti Shikshan Sansthan was founded in 1993 by Mr. Rajendra Sharma and Mrs. Manju Sharma with one belief: every child deserves an education that teaches them to think deeply and critically.

Today, JVM Senior Secondary School follows the RBSE curriculum and is recognised by the Government of Rajasthan. We focus on academics, sports, arts and character so our students grow into confident, responsible citizens.
