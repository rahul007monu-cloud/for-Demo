About Our School

Jagriti Shikshan Sansthan was established in 1993 by Mr. Rajendra Sharma and Mrs. Manju Sharma with a vision to provide quality education to every child of Jhotwara.

We follow the RBSE curriculum and focus on all-round development – academics, sports, arts, and values – so our students become confident and responsible citizens.
